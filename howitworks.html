The problem with traditional learning

Most learners receive a single percentage score after a test (e.g., 61%). That number doesn't tell you which specific concepts you are struggling with. You end up studying the wrong things, wasting precious time.

GapMap solves this by diagnosing your knowledge gaps and creating a personalised path that targets the exact concepts you need to improve.

Your journey to mastery

1

Take a Diagnostic

A short, smart quiz (10-20 questions) that pinpoints your strengths and weaknesses.

2

See Your Gap Map

Your dashboard visualises your understanding. See which concepts are "Strong", need "Improvement", or are "Weak".

3

Follow Your Path

GapMap orders your weak concepts by priority. Start with the most important one first.

4

Learn & Practice

Receive simple explanations, worked examples, and targeted practice questions with immediate, detailed feedback.

5

Retest & Progress

Retest to prove your improvement. Watch your score climb from 38% to 76% and move on to the next concept.
